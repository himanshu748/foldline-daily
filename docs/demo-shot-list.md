# Foldline Daily, 45-second demo

Record this from a phone-sized viewport on the public post. Use the post that appears
in the recording. No face, no voiceover required. On-screen text can be silent.
Say Foldline. Leave the Devvit slug off the screen.

Public post: https://www.reddit.com/r/FoldlineDaily/comments/1y1uv2a/day_1_six_panels_two_corners_par_7/

| Time      | Shot                                                                                                                               |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:05 | The Reddit feed on r/FoldlineDaily. The daily post shows the Foldline splash: the name, the day, the silhouette, and Play.         |
| 0:05–0:08 | Tap Play. The board is already foldable. The hint says to drag a panel or tap Fold up. Foldline and the day stay in the corner.    |
| 0:08–0:28 | Solve with real drags. Show the fold count climbing and the silhouette filling in. One undo is enough if it stays inside the time. |
| 0:28–0:38 | The result card: folds against par, the spoiler-free grid, and Comment your result. Cut to that comment under the post.            |
| 0:38–0:45 | The leaderboard row, or the streak line, then the post title so the daily hook is visible. End on the card or the comment.         |

Keep the whole recording at or under 45 seconds. If a solve runs long, start the
recording closer to the last two folds and keep the splash, the comment, and the
leaderboard.
