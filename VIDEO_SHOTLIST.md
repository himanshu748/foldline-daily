# Video shotlist — Foldline Daily (< 60 s)

Record on a phone (or phone-sized viewport) in the public test subreddit, against the
real demo post. No staged data: play a real day's puzzle, share a real comment.

| # | Sec | Shot | Notes |
|---|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | 0–5 | Scroll the feed, stop on today's post | The silhouette, paper colour and "Day N · hook" title must be readable. Narrate: "One puzzle a day, in the feed." |
| 2 | 5–10 | Tap the post; the expanded game opens | Point out the ghost hand hinting the first fold — no tutorial text. |
| 3 | 10–25 | Solve the puzzle with real drags | Include one undo (mention it costs a fold). Keep the HUD (folds · par · timer) in frame. |
| 4 | 25–35 | The end card | Folds vs par, the distribution chart animating, streak, countdown. Narrate the honest numbers. |
| 5 | 35–42 | Tap "Share result as comment" | Cut to the posted comment under the results thread: the 🟦🟧 grid. Explain blue = closer, orange = further — nothing spoiled. |
| 6 | 42–50 | Leaderboard rows with snoovatars; flair toggle | Mention Crease / Pleat / Origami tiers are opt-in. |
| 7 | 50–57 | Community Route editor: two taps changing a hinge, live preview, "Solver-verified · par 8" | Narrate: "Sunday's puzzle is player-made; the week's top upvoted route wins." |
| 8 | 57–60 | Back to the feed on the route post | Title card: "Foldline Daily — a port of my WebXR game Foldline." |

Recording checklist:

- [ ] Public test subreddit (< 200 members), light mode and one dark-mode cutaway in shot 4 if time allows
- [ ] Sound off (default), then one clip with sound on for shot 3
- [ ] No console open, no personal info, no invented numbers anywhere
- [ ] Real taps only — every Reddit action (comment, subscribe, flair) is shown as an explicit tap
